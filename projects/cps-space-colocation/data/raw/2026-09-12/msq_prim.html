Mile Square Health Center Primary and Pediatric Care

At Mile Square Health Center, our mission is to provide access to all-inclusive physical and emotional health in all stages of life regardless of their ability to pay. Using a patient-centered, team-based approach, Mile Square primary and pediatric care providers deliver empathic, compassionate, and knowledgeable care.

Primary Care (18+)

Primary care provides accessible healthcare services by physicians and advanced practice providers, including physician assistants and nurse practitioners. Our healthcare teams address personal healthcare needs, develop ongoing partnerships with patients, and practice in the context of family and community.

  • Preventative Care
  • Disease Diagnosis, Management, and Treatment
  • Physical Exams
  • Vaccinations
  • Behavioral Health Services
  • Referrals to Specialists
  • Medication Maintenance
  • …and more!

Prioritizing Children’s Physicals

Back to school physicals help keep kids on a healthy track by ensuring vaccinations and developmental milestones are met. It’s also a great time to check-in on kids’ emotional and social concerns.

Pediatric Care (0-17)

Our healthcare team includes pediatricians and advanced practice registered nurses (APRN) providers, who address the unique concerns of children from birth to young adulthood.

  • Physical Exams
  • IL State Required Vaccinations
  • Preventative Care
  • Disease Diagnosis, Management, and Treatment
  • Behavioral Health Services
  • Referrals to Specialists
  • Medication Maintenance
  • …and more!

Both primary and pediatric specialties focus on preventative health, including how to lead a healthy lifestyle and promote overall health and wellness. Primary and pediatric care providers also diagnose and treat a variety of issues, including temporary conditions, like the flu and a sprained ankle, and chronic (ongoing) conditions, like asthma and diabetes.

In addition to meeting your family’s physical needs, primary and pediatric care providers support healthy emotional development from childhood through adulthood. They can screen for, diagnose, and, in some cases, manage mental health concerns, like anxiety and depression.

Coordinated Care

At UI Health Mile Square Health Center, we are committed to providing the care you require that is convenient and efficient. Whether you’re seeking care for your child or yourself, we can help with everything from understanding insurance coverage to finding the right provider. We can also help your child transition to adult primary care when the time comes.

Gender-affirming Care

We also support our gender-diverse patients through inclusive, comprehensive healthcare. Through the holistic lens of primary care, we offer chronic disease and preventative services as well as community-specific needs. This includes gender-affirming hormone therapy, PrEP (pre-exposure prophylaxis), reproductive health services, behavioral health services, and connection with UI Health’s gender-affirming surgical clinic 'Spectrum'.

Providers have specific clinic availability held for gender-affirming care visits, which ensures increased availability and access. Gender-affirming team members include family medicine physicians, behavioral health specialists, infectious disease specialists, gynecology providers, and midwifery specialists. When scheduling, please mention gender-affirming care to be set up with an experienced provider.

Our Team

The Mile Square primary and pediatric care team consists of experts in primary care and pediatric care who work together to meet the health needs of you and your family. Our team includes:

  • Family Medicine providers
  • Pediatricians
  • Physician Assistants
  • Advanced Practice Nurses
