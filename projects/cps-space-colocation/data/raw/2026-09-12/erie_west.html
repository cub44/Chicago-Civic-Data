Erie Westside Health Center at Laura S. Ward Elementary School serves students at the school and people of all ages in the community.

We provide a wide variety of services for adults and children, including primary care, women’s healthcare, prenatal care, pediatric care and behavioral healthcare. You can come to Erie for everything from free pregnancy tests and school physicals to cholesterol checks and diabetes management. We provide primary care and health education to promote health, prevent disease and foster healthier lifestyles in the Southwest Humboldt Park community.

Erie is available to you any time when you are sick — even when the clinic is closed. Take a look at the services listed below to see how Erie can help you and the people you love lead healthier lives.

(The health center entrance is located on the west side of the building off Ridgeway Avenue.)

Insurance We Accept Expand

Erie accepts all Medicaid plans and many private insurances.

We also offer a sliding fee payment scale based on income. For the most up-to-date information visit: erie.health/pay

Erie MyChart Expand

Use Erie’s patient portal Erie MyChart, at myeriehealth.com or in the MyChart App, to message your care team, see your lab results, download your referrals, pay your bills and more.

Ask at the front desk or call 312-666-3494 for a link to set up your account.

After Hours Care Expand

If you are an Erie patient, and you feel sick, call 847.666.3494. An on-call nurse or doctor is always available: 24 hours/day, 7 days/week.

This call may be covered by insurance, or if not covered, a sliding scale fee may apply.
